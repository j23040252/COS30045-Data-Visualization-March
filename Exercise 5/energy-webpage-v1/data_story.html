Based on the insights from the data visualizations, here are some key advice for consumers when purchasing a TV:

1. Choose ARK brand TVs because they use the least energy during operation as compared to other brands.

2. Consider the following conditions to choose the TV that most suitable for you:

a. Small to Mid-Size (24"-50"): You can choose TV using LCD (LED) technology because they tend to have higher star rating and are more cost-effective.

b. Large Screens (50"+): You can choose OLED TVs because they can achieve higher star ratings and better energy efficiency even with a larger display screen.
